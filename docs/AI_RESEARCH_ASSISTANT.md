# LegalDesk AI — AI Legal Research Assistant & Memorandum Drafter Specification

## 1. Overview

The **AI Legal Research Assistant** performs automated legal issue spotting, synthesizes multi-jurisdiction precedents, generates formal legal research memoranda, and maps supporting versus opposing authorities.

---

## 2. Argument Mapping Matrix

- **Issue Identification**: Analyzes contractual or dispute facts.
- **Supporting Authorities**: High authority-rank precedents favoring client position.
- **Opposing Authorities**: Counter-arguments & fraud exceptions to address in briefs.
