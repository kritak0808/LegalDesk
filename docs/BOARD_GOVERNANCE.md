# LegalDesk AI — Board Governance Specification

## 1. Overview

The **Board Governance Platform** manages board & committee meetings, formal resolutions, voting tallies, meeting minutes, and automated board pack compilation.

---

## 2. Resolution Voting Workflow

- **Resolution Creation**: Proposed by Corporate Secretary / General Counsel.
- **Voting Tally**: Records votes FOR, AGAINST, and ABSTAINS.
- **Status**: Transitioned to `Passed` or `Defeated` and logged in system Audit Trail.
