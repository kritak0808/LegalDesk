# LegalDesk AI — Legal Knowledge Graph Specification

## 1. Overview

The **Legal Knowledge Graph** constructs a visual node-and-edge network mapping relationships across legal entities, corporate organizations, active matters, governing statutes, and contractual clauses.

---

## 2. Graph Schema & Relationships

### Node Types
- `Contract`, `Matter`, `Organization`, `Clause`, `Regulation`, `Person`, `Department`.

### Edge Relationships
- `PARTY_TO`, `LINKED_TO`, `CONTAINS`, `REGULATED_BY`, `LEAD_COUNSEL`, `GOVERNS`.
