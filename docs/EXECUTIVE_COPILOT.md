# LegalDesk AI — AI Executive Copilot Specification

## 1. Overview

The **AI Executive Copilot** provides natural language executive Q&A ("What changed this week?", "What is our highest legal risk?", "Which contracts require executive attention?"), morning briefs, and weekly digests.

---

## 2. Executive Copilot Capabilities
- **Morning Executive Brief**: Synthesizes top portfolio metrics and pending C-suite signoffs.
- **Weekly Digest**: Turnaround time trends, outside counsel spend velocity, and risk changes.
- **Natural Language Query**: Answers questions regarding legal risk, contract approvals, and litigation exposure.
