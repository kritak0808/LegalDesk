# LegalDesk AI — Enterprise AI Contract Intelligence Platform

## 1. Overview

The **Enterprise AI Contract Intelligence Platform** transforms LegalDesk AI into an AI-first legal operating system. It automatically ingests legal documents, performs high-precision OCR, parses legal metadata, computes multi-dimensional risk scores, identifies playbook clause deviations, conducts hybrid semantic RAG searches, maps legal knowledge graphs, and assists lawyers during negotiations.

---

## 2. Core AI Pipelines

1. **Document Ingestion & OCR Pipeline**: PDF, DOCX, TXT, scanned images, multi-page OCR text extraction with bounding box coordinates and quality confidence scores.
2. **Intelligent Document Parser**: Extracts dates, governing law, jurisdiction, monetary values, indemnities, liability caps, notice periods, and termination terms.
3. **Multi-Dimensional Risk Engine**: Calculates 0-100 risk scores across Financial, Legal, Compliance, Privacy, and Vendor risk dimensions.
4. **Hybrid RAG Semantic Search Engine**: Dense vector embeddings + keyword hybrid retrieval across contracts, matters, and playbook clauses.
5. **Legal Knowledge Graph**: Connects Contracts, Matters, Organizations, Clauses, Regulations (EU AI Act, GDPR), and People into a visual relationship graph.
6. **AI Negotiation Assistant**: Generates business-friendly, legal-friendly, and fallback clause rewrites.
