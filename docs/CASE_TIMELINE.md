# LegalDesk AI — Interactive Case Timeline Specification

## 1. Overview

The **Interactive Case Timeline** aggregates case events, evidence uploads, court filings, hearing outcomes, AI risk reviews, and settlement offers into a single chronological stream.

---

## 2. Event Types & Stream

- **Case Intake**: Preliminary intake & conflict checks.
- **Filings**: Complaints, Motions, Affidavits, Court Orders.
- **Evidence Logs**: SHA256 hashed evidence uploads.
- **Hearings**: Virtual & physical court hearings.
- **Settlement Offers**: Counter-offers and approval events.
