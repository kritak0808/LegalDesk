# LegalDesk AI — Email & Calendar Intelligence Specification

## 1. Overview

The **Email Intelligence System** automatically ingests correspondence from Outlook and Gmail, classifies threads via AI, extracts attachments, and links emails directly to active Matters, Contracts, or Disputes.

---

## 2. Intelligence Capabilities

- **AI Thread Reconstruction**: Groups related correspondence into chronological streams.
- **Hearing & Calendar Sync**: Syncs court dates to Outlook/Google Calendar with conflict detection alerts.
