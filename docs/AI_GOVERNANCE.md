# LegalDesk AI — AI Governance & EU AI Act Specification

## 1. Overview

The **AI Governance Platform** maintains an enterprise Model Registry, tracks EU AI Act Article 10 classification cards, executes bias & fairness audits, and enforces human-in-the-loop signoffs.

---

## 2. EU AI Act Classification Tiers
1. **Unacceptable Risk**: Prohibited AI systems.
2. **High Risk (Article 10)**: AI models used for contract risk scoring and legal evaluations — requires data governance model cards and bias audit compliance.
3. **Limited Risk**: Transparency requirement (e.g. AI chatbots).
