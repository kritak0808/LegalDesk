# LegalDesk AI — Precedent Engine & Citation Validation Specification

## 1. Overview

The **Precedent Engine & Citation Validation System** parses legal citation strings, verifies authority validity, calculates quantitative Authority Rank Scores (0-100), and tracks judicial treatment history.

---

## 2. Judicial Treatment Matrix

- **Leading Precedent**: Primary authoritative precedent.
- **Followed**: Subsequent decisions affirming principles.
- **Distinguished**: Precedents differentiated on facts.
- **Overruled**: Superceded judicial authority.
