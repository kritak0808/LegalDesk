# LegalDesk AI — Multi-Level Approval Workflow Engine

## 1. Overview

The **Approval Workflow Engine** manages sequential and parallel multi-tier contract signoffs across Requester, Legal Counsel, Finance, Compliance, Executive, and Board levels before execution.

---

## 2. Approval Step Progression

```
[Contract Drafted] ──► Step 1: Legal Review ──► Step 2: Finance Review ──► Step 3: Executive Signoff ──► [Ready for Execution]
```

### Approval Tiers & Rules
1. **Step 1: Legal Review**: Verifies clause alignment, indemnity caps, and governing law.
2. **Step 2: Finance Review**: Verifies payment terms, currency, tax structure, and contract value thresholds.
3. **Step 3: Executive / Board Approval**: Required for contract values exceeding $1,000,000 or high-risk contracts.
