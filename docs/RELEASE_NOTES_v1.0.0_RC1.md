# LegalDesk AI — Version 1.0.0 Release Candidate 1 (v1.0.0-RC1) Release Notes

## 1. Release Highlights

LegalDesk AI **v1.0.0-RC1** is the first commercial release candidate of the Enterprise AI Legal Operating System, featuring all 13 completed phases:

1. **Enterprise Monorepo & Workspace Shell**
2. **Enterprise IAM & Multi-Tenancy Engine**
3. **Enterprise Matter Operations Workspace**
4. **Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM)**
5. **AI Contract Intelligence & RAG Platform**
6. **Litigation & Evidence Custody Vault**
7. **Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)**
8. **Legal Research & Precedent Platform**
9. **Visual Legal Workflow Automation Engine**
10. **Executive Intelligence & Spend Platform**
11. **Connected Enterprise Integrations & Webhooks**
12. **Platform Operations & SOC Security Center**
13. **Production Hardening & QA Certification**
